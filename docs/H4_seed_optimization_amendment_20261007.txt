2026-10-07 — Model-specific seed sizing and proposed pilot reuse

This supersedes the uniform-40 seed rule in H4_amendment_20261007.txt.
H4 remains six separate named-model interactions; each model's eight
N × mode cells must be balanced, but seed counts may differ across models.

At a 10-percentage-point endpoint interaction and 90% marginal power:
model                         raw requirement    target at variance x1.25
llama-3.1-8b-instruct                    35                    44
qwen3-8b                                5                     6
gemma-3-12b-it                          18                    22
mistral-small-3.1-24b-instruct           5                     6
deepseek-r1-distill-qwen-14b            40                    50
gpt-oss-120b                            2                     2

Targets use the existing model-specific q_m and Welch t approximation,
recomputed with all within-model cell variances multiplied by 1.25.
Uniform scaling leaves the Welch df factor unchanged. This is a planning
sensitivity margin, not a confidence bound or guaranteed achieved power;
five pilot seeds give uncertain variance estimates.

Pilot reuse is proposed only after identical generation conditions and
serving configuration are established and inference implications reviewed.
For an approved model, retain all five pilot episodes per cell, including
when the target is only two. In that case the actual final count is five,
not a selectively chosen subset of two. Collect only max(0,target-5) new
episodes per cell. Explicitly state this in the paper's Design subsection.

Audit finding: the selected original Llama, Qwen, Gemma, Mistral and
DeepSeek pilot data use original JSON extraction; proposed main uses
final_decision_v2. These selected pilot records are NOT silently credited
or combined with v2 trajectories. GPT pilot frozen inference sources,
prompts, environment and guard match current sources after CRLF/LF
normalization; future serving details are not fully filled in yet.
GPT reuse is therefore a candidate, not a completed merge or approval.
The separate frozen-parser-v2 Llama rerun also supplies five balanced
seeds (106-110). It is an alternate source candidate, not a silently
substituted original pilot. Qwen v2 currently has 35/40 completed episodes
and does not yet supply five balanced seeds across every cell.

Draft Design wording, conditional on reuse approval:
"Replication was balanced across N and reasoning mode within each named
model, with model-specific targets determined from pilot episode variance
inflated by 25%. For models whose generation protocol was unchanged and
whose pilot reuse was approved, all five pilot episodes per cell were
included in the final dataset and credited toward the target. Models with
changed generation protocols were not combined without an explicit
version-specific decision. Pilot results had been inspected before these
amendments; the reuse and adaptation are disclosed."

The inspected pilot also informed the H4 reframing. Identical generation
settings alone do not make its reuse independent fresh confirmatory
evidence. Combined-data adaptive inference needs review; disclose the
adaptation and consider appropriate calibration or fresh-data validation.
No claim of automatic nominal error control is made for pilot-only GPT.

Analysis caveat: the existing runner currently encodes uniform counts.
Model-specific plans and ingestion of credited pilot episodes must be
implemented and reviewed after these numbers/eligibility decisions are
approved. No new dry-run has been generated and no seed map has been
passed to run_main.py. Earlier uniform-40 dry-runs are superseded for
planning, not amended or approved for execution.
